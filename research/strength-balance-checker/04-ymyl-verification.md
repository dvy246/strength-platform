# YMYL Safety Verification: Strength Balance Checker

This document verifies YMYL compliance for the Strength Balance Checker and Guide.

## Checklist

- [x] **YMYL Risk Level**: Low (calculates relative lifting proportions and offers general strength balance advice).
- [x] **Prominent Visible Disclaimer**: Verified. Present in both the tool and the static guide:
  - Tool: `Disclaimer: These ratios are general benchmarks derived from strength training literature and population averages. Individual leverages, training focus, and injury history will naturally cause deviations.`
  - Guide: Frames advice as educational reference and encourages consultation with a fitness professional.
- [x] **No Medical/Safety Claims**: Verified. Emphasizes that ratios are informational benchmarks and does not prescribe medical physical therapy.
